# Phases 4 to 6: Radar, Factory, More, fallbacks

Changed (looks only): see CHANGELOG 0.7.5. Same buttons, keys, hotkeys, inputs and links as 0.7.4 (checked by `npm run parity`).

How to reach each state in the app:
- Radar map: `/panel`, Radar.
- Collection: click card 1. Tabs: Sources, Tasks, Receipts (click a receipt to expand), More.
- Advisor: click card 2. The latest report is open; open another to compare.
- Loose ideas: click card 3. Add a raw idea in the `+` bar.
- Factory map: Factory button, pick a prototype in the drop-down (click the trigger).
- Stage panel: click a stage card. Titles and Thumbnail show generations with Mark as chosen / Discard. Template shows the two path choices.
- Advance row: bottom of the current stage panel.
- More: click `More` (header or inside a stage). Overview, Pending, Skills and docs, Log, Knowledge, Templates, Skills in Drive.
- Error view: cannot be reached normally.
- Widths: resize the pane under 70, between 70 and 109, and 110 or more.
